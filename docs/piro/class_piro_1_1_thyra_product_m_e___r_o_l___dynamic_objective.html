Detailed Description

template<typename Real>
class Piro::ThyraProductME_ROL_DynamicObjective< Real >

ThyraProductME_ROL_DynamicObjective.

Objective contribution of one ROL time stamp, used by ROL::ReducedDynamicObjective.

ROL needs derivatives with respect to u_old, u_new, and z, whereas the model evaluator provides derivatives with respect to x, x_dot, and p. On a time stamp [t_old, t_new], with dt = t_new - t_old, the time derivative is approximated by the backward difference

The contribution of a time stamp is a weighted sum of the responses at its two ends,

with weights given by the type of response:

  • Final time (onlyFinalTime = true): for the last time stamp, all other weights are zero.
  • Time integral (onlyFinalTime = false): the response is integrated in time with the trapezoidal rule, .
  • Sum over times (see setResponseTimes()): the response is the sum of the responses at the given times t_k, which must be the initial time or ends of time stamps. is the number of occurrences of in the list and, for the first time stamp (which starts at the initial time), is the number of occurrences of the initial time; all other weights are zero. The model is evaluated exactly at the listed times t_k.

The gradients are the exact derivatives of G, including the dependence of on both u_old (factor -1/dt) and u_new (factor 1/dt). Models that do not support DgDx_dot are treated as responses that do not depend on x_dot.

Use the sum of the responses at the given times as objective.

timeStamps are the Nt time stamps of ROL::ReducedDynamicObjective (time stamp 0, which ends at the initial time, is not integrated). Each time must be, up to the tolerance of Tempus::AdjointSensitivityUtils::timeTolerance (with the tolerances of the Tempus "Sensitivities" parameters, as with the Tempus driver), the initial time or the end of one of the time stamps 1, ..., Nt-1; otherwise an exception is thrown.
