Detailed Description

Specialization of LOCA::MultiContinuation::ExtendedGroup to natural continuation.

Natural continuation corresponds to a continuation equation with given by

where is the parameter component of the predictor direction . This corresponds geometrically to constraining the nonlinear solver steps used in calculating to be orthogonal to the parameter axis. The natural constraint is represented by a LOCA::MultiContinuation::NaturalConstraint object.
