Detailed Description

template<typename S, Index M = 2>
class minitensor::Banana< S, M >

Rosenbrock's banana function as an NLS: the residual is the gradient of \( (1-x_1)^2 + 100(x_2-x_1^2)^2 \). Two-dimensional; its curved, flat-bottomed valley stresses step control. Minimum at \( (1, 1) \).
