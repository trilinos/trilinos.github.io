Detailed Description

template<typename S, Index M = 2>
class minitensor::Quadratic< S, M >

Quadratic NLS: the residual is the gradient of \( c((x_1-a)^2 + (x_2-b)^2) \). Two-dimensional, convex, single minimum at \( (a, b) \).
