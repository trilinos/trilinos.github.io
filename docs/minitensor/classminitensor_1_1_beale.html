template<typename S, Index M = 2>
class minitensor::Beale< S, M >

Beale's function \( (1.5-x+xy)^2 + (2.25-x+xy^2)^2 + (2.625-x+xy^3)^2 \). Two-dimensional with sharp curved valleys; minimum at \( (3, 0.5) \).
