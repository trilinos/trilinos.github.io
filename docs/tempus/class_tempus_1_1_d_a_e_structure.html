Detailed Description

template<class Scalar>
class Tempus::DAEStructure< Scalar >

Structure of an index-1 differential-algebraic system.

The equations f(x_dot, x, p, t) = 0 are split into differential and algebraic equations. The algebraic equations are rows of the mass matrix E = df/dx_dot that are zero, e.g. constraints solved at each time (the velocity equations of a coupled velocity-thickness ice sheet model), or Dirichlet conditions written as f_i = x_i - d_i(p,t). The structure is given by a mask in the residual space, equal to 1 on the algebraic equations and 0 elsewhere: only the equations are flagged, not the unknowns. With A = df/dx and the diagonal masks P_a and P_d = I - P_a of the algebraic and differential equations, the system has index 1 when the matrix C = P_a A + P_d E (rows of df/dx for the algebraic equations, rows of df/dx_dot for the others) is nonsingular: differentiating the algebraic equations once in time gives C x_dot = -(P_a df/dt + P_d f(0, x, p, t)), which determines x_dot. Semi-explicit DAEs, x = (x_d, x_a) with E = [E_dd 0; 0 0] and df_a/dx_a nonsingular, are the typical case.

The structure is given to Tempus with the parameter "Algebraic Equations Mask" of the "Sensitivities" sublist, a Teuchos::RCP<const Thyra::VectorBase<Scalar> > (see getDAEStructure()). It is currently used by IntegratorAdjointSensitivity, to compute the jumps of the adjoint of "Final Time" and "Sum Over Times" responses (see AdjointDAEJumpSolver). The forward integration does not use it: the forward stepper must be suited to index-1 DAEs (e.g. Backward Euler) and must not compute consistent initial conditions.
