Detailed Description

template<class Scalar>
class Tempus::AdjointDAEJumpSolver< Scalar >

Solver for the adjoint jumps of an index-1 DAE.

For a "Final Time" or "Sum Over Times" response g, the adjoint of an index-1 DAE jumps at each response time t_k by the differential components of the solution z of C^T z = dg/dx^T, C = P_a A + P_d E, with E = df/dx_dot, A = df/dx at the forward state at t_k, and P_a, P_d the masks of the algebraic and differential equations (see DAEStructure), while its algebraic components give the term -df/dp^T (P_a z) of the sensitivity (see IntegratorAdjointSensitivity).

C is not assembled. The solver uses only evaluations of the adjoint model, which returns the transposed operators: E^T = W(1,0), A^T = W(0,1), and W(alpha,1) = (alpha E + A)^T. With D_alpha = P_a + P_d/alpha, W(alpha,1) D_alpha = C^T + A^T P_d / alpha, since the algebraic rows of E are zero. Hence P = W(alpha,1) D_alpha is a preconditioner of C^T, exact as alpha -> infinity (C is the limit of the row-scaled step matrix of backward Euler as the time step goes to zero), and the solver performs the preconditioned Richardson iterations z <- z + D_alpha^{-1} W(alpha,1)^{-1} (b - C^T z), with the linear solver of the adjoint model, starting from alpha = "Initial Alpha Factor" / dt, where dt is the time step size at t_k. The iteration matrix is P^{-1} A^T P_d / alpha: whenever an iteration reduces the residual by a factor larger than "Minimum Residual Reduction", alpha is multiplied by "Alpha Growth Factor" (and the solver of W(alpha,1) is rebuilt).

Options (sublist "Algebraic Jump Solver" of "Sensitivities"):

  • "Relative Tolerance" (default: 1e-10) on max_j |r_j|/|b_j| over the columns j.
  • "Maximum Iterations Per Alpha" (default: 20)
  • "Initial Alpha Factor" (default: 1.0)
  • "Alpha Growth Factor" (default: 10.0)
  • "Maximum Alpha Increases" (default: 8)
  • "Minimum Residual Reduction" (default: 0.5)
  • "Verbose" (default: false) Print the residual at each iteration.
