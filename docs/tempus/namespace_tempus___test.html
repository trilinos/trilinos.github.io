Exact sensitivity dJ/dp (Jacobian form, x_space x num_p) of the "Time Integral" or "Sum Over Times" response of the SinCos model, whose response is g(x) = x.

For "Time Integral", J = int_{t0}^{tf} x(t) dt and dJ/dp is computed with composite 5-point Gauss-Legendre quadrature of the exact sensitivities. For "Sum Over Times", J = sum_k x(t_k) and dJ/dp = sum_k dx/dp(t_k).
